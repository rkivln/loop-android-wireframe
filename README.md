#Loop

Real connections. Nearby.

Loop is a location-aware community platform that helps people discover and connect with nearby people, events, study groups, and local help.

✨ Features

📍 Discover people and activities nearby

👥 Find people based on interests and skills

📅 Explore local events and meetups

📚 Find and join study groups

🤝 Request or offer help within the community

💬 Connect through direct messaging

📝 Create and share community posts

🔒 Privacy-focused visibility and discovery controls

Loop is a connection platform — it does not include payments, deposits, wallets, or transaction features.

🎨 Design

Loop follows a premium, mobile-first visual system inspired by modern Apple-style interfaces:

Dark, minimal UI

Glassmorphism and subtle depth

Soft blue, violet, cyan, and magenta accents

High-fidelity Figma-style layouts

Smooth micro-interactions

SVG-based icons and vector graphics

No unnecessary stock imagery

Images are used only for meaningful product content such as profiles and user-generated media.
